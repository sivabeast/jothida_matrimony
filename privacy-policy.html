2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account and matrimonial profile.
  • Show your profile to other members and suggest compatible matches.
  • Provide horoscope matching, astrology reports and astrologer consultations.
  • Enable communication such as interests, chat and bookings between members.
  • Verify identity, prevent fraud, and keep the community safe.
  • Process payments and subscriptions.
  • Send service notifications and, with your consent, updates and offers.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only as follows:

  • Other members: Your profile details are visible to other registered members so they can consider you as a match. You can hide certain fields (phone, address, family details, salary, horoscope, additional photos) using your privacy settings.
  • Astrologers: Relevant horoscope and profile details are shared with the astrologer you book, so they can prepare your report or consultation.
  • Service providers: Trusted third parties who help us operate the Service — including Google Firebase (authentication, database, storage, notifications) and our payment gateway.
  • Legal reasons: When required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of users and the public.

4. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain it to meet legal, tax, security or fraud-prevention requirements.
